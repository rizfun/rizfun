How it works

Commodity pair → bonding curve → locked Uniswap v4 pool ().

01

Pair with a commodity

Day-1 live quotes: BNB, XAUt, PAXG. Your meme bonds against that quote from day one. The directory lists metals, energy, and agriculture as Soon until mints land.

02

Ride the curve

Constant-product bonding curve from ~$5k to ~$35k. Fixed 1B supply. Trade in the chosen commodity until graduation.

03

Graduate to locked Uniswap v4

At the endpoint, reserves seed a Uniswap v4 full-range pool on BSC. Post-grad fees go through the v4 hook. LP is locked permanently. .
