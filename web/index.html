How it works

Commodity pair → bonding curve → graduated locked pool.

01

Day-1 live quotes: BNB, XAUt, PAXG. Your meme bonds against that quote — gold or rice energy from day one. More commodities unlock as the registry expands.

Constant-product bonding curve from ~$5k to ~$35k. Fixed 1B supply. Trade in the chosen commodity until graduation.
